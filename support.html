Support

Help with Edge Music for Mac and Windows.

Contact: gzvsdev@icloud.com

For a problem, please include your macOS (or Windows) version and Edge Music version, what happened, the steps to make it happen again, and a screenshot if you can. For purchase or restore issues, describe what happens when you choose Restore Purchases. Never send your Apple Account password or payment details.

The free trial and buying Edge Music (Mac)

Edge Music is free to download with a 14-day free trial of everything. When the trial ends, buy Edge Music once to keep playing. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Your playlists, skins and settings stay either way.

Bought it already, or on a new Mac? Choose Edge Music › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

Which files does it play? On the Mac: MP3, AAC/M4A, Apple Lossless, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and CAF, plus MP4, MOV and M4V videos on the Mac.

The tag editor says it can't change a song. On the Mac, Edge Music can change songs in your Music folder straight away. For songs elsewhere, macOS asks once which folder Edge Music may change: choose the folder and click Allow.

Look Up Online finds nothing. Try fewer words, for example just the album name, or "Artist — Album". It needs an internet connection.

The widgets say "Nothing playing". Play a song in Edge Music; the widgets update within a moment.

Skins: skins (.edgeskin files) work on both Mac and Windows. Double-click one to install it.
