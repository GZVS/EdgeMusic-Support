Privacy Policy

Edge Music for Mac and Edge Music for Windows · Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: Edge Music collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your music, playlists and settings stay on your computer.

What stays on your computer

Your songs, library, playlists, skins, equalizer settings and everything else you set up are stored only on your own computer, in Edge Music's own folder. We (the developer) never receive any of it. On the Mac, the desktop widgets read the song that's playing from a folder shared only between Edge Music and its widgets, on the same Mac.

The tag editor changes a song file only when you click Save, and Undo can put the previous tags back.

What is sent over the internet

Edge Music works offline. It only goes online when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Look Up Online (tag editor): when you search, the words in the search box (usually the artist and album) are sent to MusicBrainz, a free music encyclopedia run by the MetaBrainz Foundation. When you pick an album, its track list is requested from MusicBrainz and its cover from the Cover Art Archive. Nothing else is sent: no file names, no information about your library, no identifiers. Like any website, these services see your internet address when they answer. Their own policies apply: MetaBrainz privacy policy and Internet Archive terms and privacy.
  • Purchases and the free trial are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (Mac) or the Microsoft Store (Windows). We never see your payment details or Apple ID; we only learn from the store that a purchase exists, so Edge Music can unlock itself.
  • Links you click, such as the optional donation link in Edge Music for Windows, open in your web browser. If you choose to donate, PayPal's privacy policy applies.

Children

Edge Music doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we don't collect or keep any data about you, there's nothing to request, export or delete from us. To remove everything Edge Music keeps on your computer, delete the app and its folder (on the Mac: ~/Library/Containers/com.zacharidis.EdgeMusic).

Changes

If Edge Music ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
